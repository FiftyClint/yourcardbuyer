Before you send

Photos that get a better offer

An offer can only account for what the photos show. Condition drives price, so show it.

Singles and slabs

Front and back of the cards that matter most. On a slab, the label and cert number should be readable.

Binders and boxes

One wide shot of everything, then a few pages or rows up close so the names and years can be read.

Sealed product

Every side of the box, including the shrink wrap and any seal stickers.

Light

Daylight or a bright room, without flash. Glare hides corners, edges and surface wear.

Questions

Before you send anything

Do I have to sell if I ask for an offer?

No. Asking costs nothing, and the offer is yours to take or turn down.

How do you decide what to offer?

Offers are based on what the same cards in similar condition have recently sold for. Photos of the front, back and corners are what that judgment is made from.

Do my cards need to be graded?

No. Raw cards are fine. If some are in slabs, include a photo of each label.

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

What we collect

When you ask for an offer we collect what you send: your name, email address, phone number and ZIP code if you give them, your description of the cards, any price you name, and the photos you upload. Our hosting provider records basic technical information, such as the type of browser that loaded a page. We do not use it to identify you.

We never ask for bank or card numbers through this site, and you should not send them.

What we do with it

We use your submission to look at your cards, make an offer, and contact you about it. We do not sell your information or share it for anyone else's marketing.

This is the consent shown above the button on the form:

By sending this form you agree that Your Card Buyer may call or email you about the cards you described.

Texting is separate. We only text you if you tick the box that says: "You may text me about my cards at the number above. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out." Reply STOP to any text and we stop.

Who else handles it

Submissions and photos are stored in our customer management system, which a software company runs for us, and the site is hosted by a web hosting company. Both handle your information only to provide that service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep submissions while they are useful for the offer and our purchase records, and delete them when they are not. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or tell us to stop contacting you. Reply to any message from us, or use the contact details on the home page.
